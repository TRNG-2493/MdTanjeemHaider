(a) I used a set for seen_ids because sets do not keep duplicates. I used a dict for lookup because I wanted to get the whole case by its ID with just 1 step (like lookup["TC-107"]).

(b) If lookup was a list I would have to for loop through every single case to find the one I want. This is okay if the amount of cases is small but in a scenario where there is A LOT of cases, it would be slow. Dict on the other hand lets me lookup instantly (O(1)).
